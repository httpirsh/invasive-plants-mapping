# Research Questions

> **Status: preliminary.** These questions reflect the current
> understanding of the problem at the start of the thesis. They are
> expected to be refined once the citizen science and satellite
> datasets have been explored and the relevant literature reviewed.
>
> See [`research_gaps.md`](research_gaps.md) for a set of candidate,
> more specific research questions (RQ1–RQ5) derived from a first
> pass at directly relevant literature, and for how they relate to —
> and still need to be reconciled with — the questions below.

## Main Research Question

Can Earth Observation Foundation Models improve the mapping of
invasive alien plants when combined with citizen science observations
and satellite imagery?

## Secondary Questions

### RQ1 — Data

How can citizen science observations and satellite imagery be
effectively integrated for invasive alien plant mapping?

Related open questions: [Citizen Science vs. Satellite Data](methodology.md#citizen-science-vs-satellite-data), [Data Quality and Bias](methodology.md#data-quality-and-bias), [Dataset Construction](methodology.md#dataset-construction).

### RQ2 — Representation

Do representations extracted from Earth Observation Foundation Models
provide useful information for identifying invasive alien plants?

Related open questions: [Foundation Model Philosophy](methodology.md#foundation-model-philosophy).

### RQ3 — Model Performance

Do foundation-model representations improve predictive performance
compared with conventional satellite-derived features?

Related open questions: [Baseline Philosophy](methodology.md#baseline-philosophy), [Foundation Model Philosophy](methodology.md#foundation-model-philosophy), [Evaluation Principles](methodology.md#evaluation-principles).

### RQ4 — Generalization

How well do the developed models generalize to spatially independent
areas?

Related open questions: [Validation Strategy](methodology.md#validation-strategy), [Ablation and Generalization](methodology.md#ablation-and-generalization).
